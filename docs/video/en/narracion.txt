intro: PRC AI 2.0. An artificial intelligence in the Windows terminal, on your own computer.
local: The model, Gemma 4, runs on the laptop itself. No key, no cloud, and the conversation never leaves your computer.
web: When a question needs current data, it searches the internet, reads the pages and cites its sources.
equipo: And what the computer already knows, like its free memory, it reads straight from Windows.
comandos: You can ask for a new command just by chatting. Read-only: fixed rules check it, it tests it and saves it. From then on, it uses it when you ask.
consultas: Web pages work the same way: it picks the page, pulls out the data you ask for, and shows it to you before saving.
preferencias: It also remembers how you want it to answer, in this conversation and the next ones.
aprende: And the newest part: it learns from your business folder. It reads Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF, and tells you what it can't read, like a scanned invoice.
pregunta: You ask, and it answers with what your documents say, and which one it comes from: a whole carrot cake is 48 dollars, according to the PDF catalog. And if something isn't there, like an employee's pay, it doesn't make it up: it tells you it's not in the documents.
buscar: With documents search you can see where each answer comes from, without asking the model. Here, the price table from the PDF.
olvida: It also forgets. Ask it to forget the extensions: it shows what it will forget and waits for your yes. Your files aren't touched, and in a new conversation that data is gone.
recuerda: And it can be undone: what was forgotten stays on a list, and restore brings it back.
final: PRC AI 2.0. It learns from your business documents, forgets what you ask it to, and if something isn't there, it tells you.
